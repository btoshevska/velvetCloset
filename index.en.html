VELVET CLOSET · EST. 2026

We are Elena and Bojana, two girls who share a love for fashion, unique clothing pieces, and discovering hidden fashion treasures.

The idea behind VELVET CLOSET started with our desire to create something of our own – something creative and different. We believe every piece of clothing has its own story, and you don't always need to buy something new to look amazing.

Our goal is to offer unique, trendy, and affordable pieces that let you express your personal style and feel special.

VELVET CLOSET is more than a second-hand store – it's a story about our love for fashion, creativity, and a more sustainable lifestyle.

Our story

VELVET CLOSET began in April 2026 with one simple idea – to give clothing a new life and prove that great style doesn't always mean buying something new.

We believe the most beautiful fashion pieces are those with character, a story, and a unique charm. We created a space where individuality meets affordability and every piece gets the chance to become part of someone's new story.

Our inspiration comes from our love for fashion, carefully selected pieces, and the belief that true style lies in the details, not the price.

By wearing clothes again, we can build better habits for the environment and appreciate the value of what already exists.
